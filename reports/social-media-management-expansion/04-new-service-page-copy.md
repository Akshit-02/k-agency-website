## What is social media management?
Social media management is the ongoing work of running a brand's social accounts: deciding what to say and where, planning a content calendar, creating posts and short videos, publishing them, responding to the community and reviewing what worked. Kudozz does this month by month, and can connect it with creator partnerships and UGC when your plan needs them.

## Business problem — "Most brand accounts don't fail. They just drift."
Social media rarely goes wrong in one big moment. It slips: a missed week, a rushed post, a comment left unanswered. These are the patterns we see most often when brands first talk to us.

1. **Posting when someone finds time.** A burst of posts before a sale, then silence for three weeks. Followers notice the gaps.
2. **No plan behind the posts.** Content gets made because the calendar is empty, not because it supports anything the business needs.
3. **Every post looks the same.** The same product shot and the same caption format, month after month, until people scroll past by habit.
4. **Reels that never get made.** Everyone agrees short video matters. Nobody has the time to script, shoot and edit it every week.
5. **Comments nobody answers.** Questions sit unanswered under posts, and the people who did engage stop bothering.
6. **Too many platforms, too few hands.** Instagram, LinkedIn, YouTube and Facebook each want something different, and one person is expected to do it all.
7. **No calendar anyone can see.** Launches, festive moments and offers get remembered the night before, so content is rushed or missed.
8. **Reports that say nothing.** A screenshot of follower count each month, with no view on what to do differently next month.
9. **Social sitting apart from the business.** Social activity runs on its own track, disconnected from sales, launches and what the founder is actually trying to grow.

## Our approach — "Strategy first, then a calendar, then the content."
Posting is the visible part. Most of the work happens before and after it. Every month runs through the same loop, and each loop gets sharper because it learns from the last one.

1. **Understand the brand and its audience.** What you sell, who buys it, how you want to sound and what your customers already say about you.
2. **Study competitors and the category.** What others in your space post, what gets a response, and where there is room to stand apart.
3. **Set objectives for each platform.** Awareness on one, education or enquiries on another. Each platform gets a job, not the same post.
4. **Build the content strategy.** Content pillars, formats, tone and a posting rhythm your team can realistically approve.
5. **Plan the monthly calendar.** Every post, Reel and Story mapped against launches, offers, festive dates and creator activity.
6. **Create and review content.** Designs, scripts, edits and captions, shared with you for approval before anything goes live.
7. **Schedule and publish.** Approved content goes out on time, formatted properly for each platform.
8. **Manage the community.** Comments and messages handled within the agreed scope, with anything sensitive passed to your team.
9. **Review and improve.** A monthly look at what worked and what didn't, which shapes the next calendar.

_Then the next month's calendar starts from what step nine taught us._

## FAQs
**1. What does social media management include?**
At Kudozz it covers strategy, monthly content planning, creative design, short-form video scripting and editing, copywriting, scheduling and publishing, community management within an agreed scope, and a monthly performance report. The exact mix, platforms and number of posts are agreed in your proposal.

**2. Which social media platforms does Kudozz manage?**
We plan content for Instagram, Facebook, LinkedIn, YouTube, Pinterest, X and Snapchat. Most brands don't need all of them. We recommend platforms based on your audience, business model, the content you can realistically produce and your goals, and the agreed platforms are listed in your scope.

**3. Is social media strategy included?**
Yes. Every engagement starts with an audit of your accounts and competitors, then a strategy covering platforms, content pillars, tone and posting rhythm. We revisit it each quarter as we learn what works for your audience.

**4. Does Kudozz create Instagram Reels?**
Yes. We plan Reels as series, write scripts and hooks, and edit from footage you share or footage creators shoot for you. If your Reels need a professional or on-location shoot, we scope that separately.

**5. Does Kudozz design social media posts and carousels?**
Yes. Static posts, carousels, infographics, promotional and product creatives are designed in line with your brand guidelines and shared for approval before publishing.

**6. How often will content be published?**
It depends on the platforms, your goals and how much content you can approve each month. We'd rather agree a rhythm you can keep than promise daily posting that drops off. The number of posts, Reels and Stories per platform is set in your scope.

**7. Does Kudozz manage comments and messages?**
We monitor comments and handle routine interactions within the hours and scope we agree, and pass anything sensitive, such as complaints, order issues or medical and legal questions, to your team. Full customer support and 24/7 moderation are separate requirements.

**8. Does social media management include paid advertising?**
No. Social media management covers your organic presence. Paid social advertising can be discussed and scoped separately, and any ad budget is paid by you to the platform.

**9. Can Kudozz manage social media for a D2C brand?**
Yes. D2C brands are a natural fit because product education, launches, offers and creator content all need to work together. We plan your calendar around drops, sales and festive moments, and can bring creator and UGC content into it.

**10. Can Kudozz combine social media management with influencer marketing?**
Yes, and this is where we're different from most social media agencies. One team can plan your own content and your creator campaigns together, so launches are coordinated and creator content, where rights allow, can be reused on your channels. Influencer fees are quoted separately.

**11. How is social media performance measured?**
We agree what matters for each platform at the start, such as reach, engagement, video views, profile activity, website clicks or enquiries. Each month we report on these, explain which content performed and why, and recommend changes. Metrics differ by platform, and website and enquiry tracking needs to be set up on your side.

**12. How much does social media management cost?**
There's no fixed package price. Cost depends on the number of platforms, how many posts and videos you need each month, whether creator content is involved, community management hours and any shoots. We share a scope and fee in the proposal after the first call.

**13. How long does it take to build a consistent social media presence?**
Consistency is visible from the first month, because a planned calendar replaces ad hoc posting. Building an audience that recognises and engages with your brand takes longer and depends on your category, content and starting point. We review progress monthly rather than promising a timeline for growth.

**14. What does a brand need to provide before starting?**
Brand guidelines or examples of what you like, product and service information, access to your social accounts or insights, upcoming launches and offers, any footage or images you already have, and a named person who approves content.
