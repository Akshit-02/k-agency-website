## Implications for Kudozz's own operations (Strategic notes)
1. **Per-market label lists in every brief.** One "#ad" policy doesn't work everywhere.
2. **No permit or licence claims.** Never state or imply that Kudozz holds or can obtain UAE, Saudi or other licences for creators. Kudozz checks creator status instead (as the site now says).
3. **Data.** Creator applications and brand inquiries collect personal data. Review the privacy policy against the DPDP Rules timeline (to about May 2027) with counsel. The privacy policy was not changed.
4. **Tax content.** Several creator articles still cite old section numbers (194R, 194J) with a note on the 2025 Act. Have a CA confirm the s.393 table entries before mass-updating.
