# 07 — International Content Approach

## Principle applied

India is the primary market for every article. No country variants were created, and no thin international pages. Regional differences are covered inside an article only where they materially change the advice. Kudozz's SMM service is described as available to brands in India; no article claims offices, teams or SMM delivery outside India.

## What was deliberately not done

- No "social media management agency in UAE / UK / Australia / Singapore / US / Europe" articles. Reasons: SMM delivery outside India is unconfirmed (see `reports/social-media-management-expansion/11-international-market-opportunities.md`), and country variants of the same guide would be thin.
- No market pricing ranges for any country.
- No legal advice. Legal points are stated as current facts with sources and a recommendation to check with counsel.

## Usefulness to readers outside India

The operational guides (01, 02, 04, 05, 06, 07, 08, 09, 10, 11, 12, 13, 14, 16, 18, 19, 20) are written so the frameworks work in any market. India-specific items (GST, ASCI, DPDP, CERT-In, festive calendars) are signposted as India-specific, so international readers can substitute their local equivalents.
