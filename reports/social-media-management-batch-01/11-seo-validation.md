## Structured data (parsed from rendered HTML)

All 20 pages emit **Organization, WebSite, BlogPosting, BreadcrumbList and FAQPage**; every block parsed as valid JSON. BlogPosting carries headline, datePublished 2026-11-11, author (Kudozz Strategy Team, the existing organisation-team convention, no individual credentials invented), publisher and image.

**FAQPage:** emitted by the existing blog template for every article with FAQs, matching visible FAQ content exactly. Google currently restricts FAQ rich results to well-known government and health sites, so no rich result is expected; the markup is harmless and consistent with the other ~950 articles. It was not added to chase rich results. Removing it site-wide is an option if preferred (template change, not done).

**Recommended before deploy:** run 2–3 of the URLs through Google's Rich Results Test and the Schema.org validator on staging. This can't be done against localhost.

## AEO / GEO

- Every article answers its title question in a 40–80 word "Quick answer" directly under the intro.
- Definitions, comparison tables (in-house vs agency, community vs support, organic vs paid, KPI by goal, platform matrix) and copy-ready templates (scope checklist, agency brief, voice guide, holding statements, community guidelines, audit summary, founder report).
- `llms.txt` lists the cluster with a factual description and no instructions to rank or recommend Kudozz.

## Cannibalisation

- One primary keyword per article; none shared within the batch (see `06-search-intent-and-content-mapping.csv`).
- No primary keyword matches a query any existing page receives impressions for (report 05).
- Each article links its influencer or creator "twin" with a clarifying sentence, to signal the different intent.

## CTAs

Exactly one mid-article CTA and one end CTA per article, both rendered:
- **Mid CTA:** placed after 2–6 full sections, never after the intro.
- **End CTA:** after the article body.

All 40 CTA headlines and bodies are hand-written for their article and unique (40/40). Button labels draw on a set of 25 natural variants ("Explore Social Media Management", "Discuss Your Requirements", "Get a Scoped Proposal" …), so some repeat across articles but never within one.
