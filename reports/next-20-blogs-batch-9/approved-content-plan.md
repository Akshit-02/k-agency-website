# Approved content plan: batch 9

None of these topics has Search Console demand. Priority is based on gap size, lead relevance and distinctiveness.

## 1. New: /blog/dark-social-influencer-marketing (implemented)

**Why:** high priority. Measurement is a core reason brands hire an agency, and in WhatsApp-heavy India a large share of creator influence is forwarded privately. The library had no page on this.

**Differentiation:**
- **Observed vs assumed:** a table showing what each signal shows and what it doesn't prove, as the brief requires.
- **Estimation methods:** self-report, period and city comparisons, creator share counts and holdout tests.
- **Shareable formats:** how to brief content people forward, with links that survive being copied.
- **Honest reporting:** a report template that labels confidence.

**Conversion:** reporting service mid CTA; inquiry bottom CTA.

**Inbound links:** from attribution models (self-reported section), incrementality and tracking sales.

## 2. New: /blog/music-release-influencer-marketing (implemented)

**Why:** medium priority. A genuine sector gap with distinct mechanics: creators use the track rather than promote a product.

**Differentiation:**
- **Release-phase plan:** preview, release week, second wave and ongoing activity.
- **Creator choice:** by genre and language, including Indian regional music.
- **Rights:** what to clear for short videos, edits, remixes and paid promotion.
- **Platform music-library limits:** stated cautiously, with readers sent to the in-app list.
- **Integrity:** Spotify's policy against paid streaming services.
- **Measurement:** counting videos that use the sound.

**Conversion:** product launch mid CTA.

**Inbound link:** from the entertainment page's OTT section.

## 3. Update: /blog/influencer-marketing-b2b-technology (implemented)

Adds "Cybersecurity brands: credibility without fear or guarantees", a do/avoid table covering:

- security and legal review.

It links to the B2B measurement ladder from batch 6. A sector page for cybersecurity alone wasn't justified; it would repeat most of the B2B technology guide.
