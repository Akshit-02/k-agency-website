- The site's own `instagram-influencer-rates-india` and `youtube-influencer-rates-india` publish no rupee ranges (they explain pricing qualitatively); neither was edited.

## Research limitations (read before publishing)

- **Three of the seven sources (upGrowth, Grynow, StarClinch) are agency or marketplace blogs**, not independent research. They are commercial estimates, and two of them publish near-identical agency-fee ranges. The articles attribute them by name and call them indicative, but treat them as directional. Independent research (Kofluence via Campaign India, Goat/Kantar via IBEF, WPP Media) is used for market-level figures.
- Tier thresholds differ by source (e.g. where "macro" starts). Article #55 shows that disagreement in a comparison table instead of picking one.
- No primary data from Kudozz's own campaigns was available or used.
- Market-size figures come from different methodologies and years (₹3,600 crore for 2024 vs ₹3,000–3,500 crore for 2025) and are presented with their own sources rather than reconciled.
