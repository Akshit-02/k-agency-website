Continuation of the Discovery cluster (reports for batches 1 and 2 in `reports/kudozz-discovery-blogs/` and `reports/kudozz-discovery-blogs-batch-2/`).

## Method and limits

- Primary keywords are from the brief. Secondary keywords and questions come from the GSC export (`gscReport/Queries.csv`), the old versions of the posts being upgraded (which show what each URL already ranks for), and the questions a brand asks at each stage after discovery.
- **No search volumes, difficulty scores or traffic projections**; none were available and none were invented. Pricing figures are only used where attributable to a dated source (see report 04).
- For URLs that already earn impressions, the existing core query is kept in the H1 or SEO title so the rewrite doesn't lose it.

## Cluster role

Batch 3 covers what happens **after discovery**: evaluation (41–45), outreach (46–50), pricing (51–55) and execution (56–60). #60 is the bridge from the discovery cluster (articles 1–40) into these stages. Kudozz Discovery is positioned only where it helps: finding, filtering and comparing creators and revealing contact details with credits. It is never presented as an outreach, pricing, CRM or campaign-management tool.
