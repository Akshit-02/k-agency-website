# 02 — Location Opportunity Matrix

**Metrics policy:**
- No keyword tool was available, so **search volume, difficulty and CPC are not measured** and not estimated.
- GSC has **0 impressions** for this keyword family in every location (see 01).
- Priorities are therefore **qualitative**, based on:
  - SERP intent (checked for Mumbai and Ahmedabad);
  - the size of the business audience (major metros vs smaller cities);
  - Kudozz service fit (SMM confirmed for India only);
  - competitor availability (Clutch and DesignRush show many SMM agencies in each wave-1 metro);
  - differentiation potential;
  - existing Kudozz location performance (national > state > city);
  - cannibalisation and doorway risk.

**Priority key:**
- **P1:** build in wave 1.
- **P2:** build after wave-1 review, or after a business confirmation.
- **P3:** research further.
- **Excluded:** don't build now.
