# 07 — Publishing Roadmap (Location SEO)

Nothing has been published. With no draft flag in the codebase, release in waves by keeping later arrays commented out in `src/content/location-guides/index.ts`, and set `publishedAt` to the real release date.

## Before any release
1. Ship `/services/social-media-management` and the Batch 01–03 guides the location pages link to.
2. Re-verify the 36 competitor records; agency sites change.
3. Decide on the outbound-link policy (domains as text vs nofollow links).
4. Decide on the existing doorway-risk pages (the 30 specialist influencer city listicles).

## Wave 1 schedule (recommended)

| Week | Pages | Why this order |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | India (hub) | The national listicle is the strongest location format on this site (the influencer India page has 14,204 impressions); it links every city page |
| 2 | Mumbai, Delhi NCR, Bengaluru | Largest metro business audiences |
| 3 | Ahmedabad, Pune, Hyderabad | Distinct business mixes (manufacturing, IT, pharma) |
| 4 | Chennai, Kolkata | Complete wave 1 |

Request indexing after each week and spot-check the live pages.

## Review gate (8–12 weeks after week 4)

Export GSC and check, per page:
- impressions for the four keyword variants
- average position and CTR
- whether "marketing" and "management" queries both land on the same page, which validates consolidation

| Signal | Next step |
|---|---|
| Wave-1 pages earn impressions | Build P2 Indian cities one at a time (Jaipur, Surat, Indore, Chandigarh, Kochi, Lucknow, Coimbatore, Vadodara), each with fresh local research and different competitors |
| Little or no impressions after 12 weeks | Don't expand. Improve the India hub and consider folding weak city pages into it (needs approval) |
| SMM delivery confirmed for a market | UAE, then Saudi Arabia, UK, Australia and Singapore country pages, using local English and honest remote-service wording, and Arabic capability stated accurately |
| "Management" queries land on a different page type | Revisit consolidation, and only split if the SERPs show distinct intent |

## Not planned
- State-level SMM pages.
- Gurugram, Noida and Faridabad pages (covered by Delhi NCR).
- City service landing pages, unless Kudozz opens a local presence.
- Smaller GCC markets, until demand and delivery are confirmed.
