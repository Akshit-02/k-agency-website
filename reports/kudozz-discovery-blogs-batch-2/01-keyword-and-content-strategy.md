# 01 — Keyword and content strategy (batch 2, articles 21–40)

Continuation of `reports/kudozz-discovery-blogs/01-keyword-and-content-strategy.md`.

## Method and limits

- Primary keywords are from the brief. Secondary keywords and questions come from the GSC export (`gscReport/Queries.csv`), the wording of adjacent existing posts, and what the product can actually do.
- **No search volumes, difficulty scores or traffic projections**: no keyword tool data was available, and none was invented.
- A live SERP review was not run for every keyword; writers checked official platform sources where articles state platform facts (LinkedIn, YouTube Shorts, podcasts, gaming platforms). A keyword-tool pass before publishing would sharpen titles.

## How batch 2 extends the cluster

- **Advanced discovery (21–25)** sits under the batch-1 pillar `find-indian-influencers` and the relevance guide `influencer-search-tools`, adding audience, emergence, funnel, language and niche depth.
- **Industry (26–30)** joins beauty, fashion, D2C and local from batch 1.
- **Platforms (31–35)** joins `how-to-find-instagram-influencers` and `how-to-find-youtube-influencers`.
- **Campaign sourcing (36–40)** extends the batch-1 workflow chain (research → checks → shortlist → outreach list → database → checklist) with multi-city, ambassador, seasonal, seeding and the repeatable workflow.
