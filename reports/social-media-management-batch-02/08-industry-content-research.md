## Industry-specific insights used

| Industry | What makes social media management different | Compliance and trust points | Existing influencer twin (cross-linked) |
|---|---|---|---|
| D2C (36) | Product education and customer questions do the selling; COD, returns, sizing and "is this the same as on Amazon?" questions need an approved answer bank; launches and festive sales concentrate effort; social sales show up on websites and marketplaces, so attribution is directional | ASCI Code (claims must be substantiated); CCPA dark-patterns guidelines (Nov 2023: false urgency, drip pricing, bait and switch) apply to festive creatives | influencer-marketing-d2c-brands-india, ugc-marketing-d2c-brands |
| Beauty and skincare (37) | Education over hype: ingredients without efficacy claims, honest demonstrations in real light, routines, real people (creators and UGC, disclosed); reported reactions are escalated to the brand, never diagnosed in comments | ASCI Code (substantiation); ASCI influencer do's and don'ts (disclosure labels, gifts as material connection, no filters that enhance the benefit); ASCI health-influencer qualification addendum; ASCI fairness-category guideline exists (read before comparison visuals); CDSCO Cosmetics Rules 2020 and the Drugs and Cosmetics Act 1940 named, with legal limits deferred to regulatory or legal teams (the CDSCO FAQ wasn't readable) | influencer-marketing-beauty-brands-india, ugc-beauty-skincare |
| Fashion (38) | Drop-led rhythm (tease, reveal, launch, sustain) plus everyday styling between drops; honest fit and fabric content cuts returns-related complaints; size and return questions go to support; Indian wedding, festive and end-of-season moments | ASCI guideline on labelling synthetic (AI-generated) content noted for AI imagery; usage rights for creator and UGC content | influencer-marketing-fashion-brands-india |
| Restaurants and cafés (39) | Local discovery (Instagram location tags, Google Business Profile posts and customer photos as a related channel); phone-friendly food video; team stories; events and offers with clear prices; customer posts reshared only with permission and credit | Allergen, hygiene and illness concerns are escalated to management with a template reply. FSSAI and allergen labelling rules are deliberately not stated (not verified) | restaurant-cafe-influencer-marketing-india |
| SaaS (40) | Trust built through education: use cases, feature explainers, short demos of one task; LinkedIn company page plus founder voice; YouTube for walkthroughs; customer stories only with written approval | LinkedIn admin roles for agency access; company page vs founder personal brand table (ownership, goals, tone, approval) | saas-influencer-marketing-india, linkedin-thought-leadership-marketing |
