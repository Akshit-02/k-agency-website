# 09 — International Content Notes (Batch 02)

India is the primary market for every article. No country variants and no international landing pages were created. Kudozz's SMM service is described only as available to brands in India; no article claims offices or teams elsewhere.

## Where international differences matter in this batch

| Topic | Difference | How it's handled |
|---|---|---|
| Platform availability | TikTok is blocked in India (covered in Batch 01 #15) but is part of the mix in the UK, Australia and the UAE | Instagram and YouTube articles don't add TikTok; they link Batch 01's platform-choice guide for the market question |
| Pinterest commerce features | Catalogs and shopping aren't available in every region; India's status couldn't be confirmed officially; Pinterest lists India under advertising partners rather than direct ad-account setup | Stated plainly in article 29 as "unconfirmed", with readers pointed to Pinterest's availability list |
| Instagram commerce features | Shopping and product tagging availability varies by market | Left out of articles 21–25 and 36 rather than stated wrongly |
| Advertising and claims rules | India: ASCI Code, ASCI influencer guidelines, CCPA guidelines, Cosmetics Rules. Elsewhere: UK ASA/CAP and CMA, UAE NMA, Australia AANA and ACCC, Singapore ASAS | India rules are cited where relevant (24, 34, 35, 36, 37, 38). International readers are pointed to the existing `influencer-disclosure-rules-by-country` guide rather than given partial summaries |
| Language | Hinglish and regional languages in India; Arabic in GCC markets | Covered in Batch 01's brand voice guide, which is linked. No translation claims made |
| Seasonality | Indian festive and wedding seasons; lunar festival dates shift every year | The calendar article (31) lists festivals generically and tells readers to check dates each year. International teams can swap in their own retail calendars (the existing `seasonal-influencer-marketing-uk` is linked from Batch 01 context, not repeated) |
| Restaurants | Food safety and allergen rules are country-specific | Not stated. Concerns are escalated to management. No FSSAI, FSA or other claims made |

## Usefulness outside India

The platform guides (21–30) describe global platform features, dated November 2026, and work in any market where those platforms are available. The content guides (31–35) are market-neutral. The industry guides (36–40) use Indian examples, such as COD, marketplaces and festive sales, but their frameworks (launch sequences, answer banks, escalation, company page vs founder) transfer directly.
