## 6. Platform research findings

- **Verified and dated (November 2026):**
  - Instagram: carousels up to 20 items, bio up to 150 characters, up to three pinned posts, Contact and action buttons, Stories stickers and insights, Trial Reels, "views" replacing plays and impressions, the originality guidance, alt text.
  - LinkedIn: admin roles, Showcase Pages (up to 25), document posts, the My Company and Employee Advocacy tabs discontinued from November 2024.
  - YouTube: Shorts up to 3 minutes for uploads after 15 October 2024, chapter rules, custom-thumbnail verification.
  - Pinterest: website claiming, visual search, and catalog regional limits (India unconfirmed).
- **Facts left out** where official pages were unreadable or conflicting: bio link count, Story length, feed aspect ratio, Reels length, posting frequency and others (full list in report 07).
- Instagram and some Meta help pages were confirmed from official mirrors or indexed text rather than full-page reads. Those claims are dated and point readers to the current help page.

## 7. Industry-specific insights

See report 08. Each industry guide is about brand-account management and cross-links its influencer twin.
- **Beauty:** sticks to the ASCI Code and influencer guidelines, names the Cosmetics Rules, and makes no efficacy claims.
- **Restaurants:** escalates safety and allergen concerns to management and states no food-law rules.
- **D2C and fashion:** honest offers in line with the CCPA guidelines on misleading ads and dark patterns.
- **SaaS:** separates company-page content from the founder's personal brand.
