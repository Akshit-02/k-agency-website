## AEO / GEO

- Each article answers its question in a 40–80 word "Quick answer" near the top.
- Tables, frameworks and copy-ready templates support answer extraction.
- Platform facts are attributed and dated.
- `llms.txt` lists the new guides with a factual summary, and no instructions to AI systems.

## Cannibalisation

- Primary keywords are unique within Batch 02 and against Batch 01. Blog 32's keyword was narrowed so it no longer competes on the generic "social media content ideas" phrase.
- Reframes 30, 31, 32 and 35 keep these articles distinct from Batch 01 and existing UGC and customer-insight pages (reports 02 and 03).
- Article 22 (strategy) and 33 (monthly planning) cross-link with explicit scope statements.
- Article 36 is the intended owner of the existing GSC query "instagram content strategy for d2c beauty brand"; 37 links to it.

## CTAs

- **Mid-article CTA:** exactly one per article, placed after 4–5 full sections. Never after the intro.
- **End CTA:** exactly one per article, after the body.
- **Copy:** headlines and bodies are hand-written and unique across both batches. Button labels vary.
