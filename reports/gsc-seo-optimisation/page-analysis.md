# Page Analysis

**Source:** the "Pages" sheet: 697 rows, all on `www.kudozz.in`, no duplicates.

- **Fragment rows:** 44 rows are `#fragment` URLs (jump-link sitelinks such as `/blog/instagram-subscriptions#free-access`). They are left out of the page totals below, which gives **653 unique pages**.
- **No clicks:** 389 of those 653 pages had impressions but **no clicks**.
- **No period comparison:** the export is a single 3-month period, so page-level decline can't be measured. At site level, traffic is rising: clicks rose from 92 (5–24 Sep) to 1,076 (25 Sep–14 Oct) to 1,548 (15 Oct–3 Nov).

## Concentration risk

`/blog/instagram-subscriptions` has **1,393 of 2,716 clicks (51%)** and 24,613 impressions. It is a creator-side guide whose top queries are about viewing paid content for free. The page already answers that honestly, with a safety warning, so it is low-value traffic for a brand-side agency. A single Instagram feature or SERP change could halve sitewide clicks. Read sitewide click trends with this in mind.

Sampled pages from the invisible groups were checked live:

- They return **HTTP 200** with `index, follow` and a self-referencing canonical.
- They are in the live sitemap.
- Their median inbound internal links (6–8) match or exceed the core blog (median 4).

**No technical block was found.** Core blog posts last modified in the same 1–14 October window have impressions on 87% of pages, while brand guides from that window have them on 2%. Age is a partial confounder, because some core posts are older URLs that were updated. Even so, the gap points to Google not indexing or not surfacing these clusters ("Discovered/Crawled – currently not indexed"). It is not a sign that the pages are too new. This needs the **Page indexing report**, which isn't in this export. See the P0 item in `prioritised-action-plan.md`.
