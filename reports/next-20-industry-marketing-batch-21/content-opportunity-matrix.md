# Content opportunity matrix: batch 21 (2026-10-25)

## Method

- **Inventory:** 922 posts at the start (924 after), searched by title, slug and full text with word-boundary patterns. All prior batch reports were checked, including the distributors and retailers page (batch 17) and the life-stage page (batch 15).
- **GSC:** the 2026-10-22 export (no date range, country or query×page data).
- **Keyword volumes:** not available, so demand is described qualitatively.
- **Scores** (1-5) are editorial judgements, not forecasts. Columns:
  - **Int:** search intent and relevance
  - **Lead:** brand-lead potential
  - **Dist:** distinctiveness from Kudozz content
  - **Dem:** demand evidence
  - **Svc:** service relevance
  - **Use:** usefulness possible
