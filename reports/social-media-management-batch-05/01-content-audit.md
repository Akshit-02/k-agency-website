## 3. GSC findings (`gsc-data.xlsx`, last 3 months)

- **Queries:** 0 queries mention salon, spa, skin/derma/aesthetic, dental/dentist, doctor, physio, gym/fitness/trainer, yoga/Pilates, wellness, coach, tutor, home services, wedding/photography or vet (the "lab" and "vet" matches were unrelated: "collaboration", "perpetual"). One beauty query: "instagram content strategy for d2c beauty brand" (29 impressions).
- **Related pages:** all low-impression influencer twins: `influencer-marketing-beauty-brands-india` 59 impressions; `influencer-marketing-healthcare-brands-india` 30; `pet-influencer-marketing-india` 28 (position 19.8); `photography-creative-services-influencer-marketing-india` 20; `wedding-influencer-marketing-india` 16; `creator-coaching-business` 13. No page ranks for any Batch 05 primary keyword.
- **Implication:** there is no existing demand signal to protect or to prioritise by, and no cannibalisation risk from ranking pages. Prioritisation is qualitative (see 02).
