## Method

- **Inventory:** 917 posts at the start (919 after), searched by title, slug, headings and full text. Prior batch reports were checked.

- **Research:** three web searches (e-commerce returns rules, UGC and returns, offline/FMCG influencer measurement); see `research-sources.md`.
