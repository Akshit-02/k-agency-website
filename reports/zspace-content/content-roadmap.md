# Deliverables C and G: prioritised roadmap and next batch

Date: 2026-10-17. The full opportunity list is in `service-content-matrix.csv` (16 rows across all six ZSpace services).

## How opportunities were ranked

1. A real ZSpace service matches the reader's problem.
2. The topic sits inside Kudozz's subject, influencer and creator marketing, so the article is credible on Kudozz.
3. No existing Kudozz article owns the intent (checked against all 889 articles).
4. No ZSpace article covers the same angle (checked against all of ZSpace's posts).
5. The facts can be verified from primary sources.
6. The link would still help the reader if search rankings didn't matter.

No search-volume data was available, so demand is an editorial judgement for every row.
