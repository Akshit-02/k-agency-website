# 05 · Metadata and Heading Changes

## Metadata

| Element | Before | After |
|---|---|---|
| Title | Influencer Marketing & Social Media Agency in India \| Kudozz (60) | **Best Influencer Marketing Agency in India \| Kudozz** (50) |
| Meta description | Kudozz is an influencer marketing and social media management agency in India: creator campaigns, brand social media, UGC and reporting tied to your goals. (155) | **Looking for the best influencer marketing, UGC or social media marketing agency in India? Kudozz runs creator campaigns, UGC videos and brand social media.** (155) |
| OG / Twitter title and description | Same as before | Same as after (generated by `buildMetadata`) |
| Canonical | `https://www.kudozz.in` | Unchanged |
| Robots | index, follow | Unchanged |
| OG image | `/opengraph-image` | Unchanged |
| Organization `description` (`siteConfig.description`, also the sitewide default description) | "…an influencer marketing and social media management agency in India…" | "Kudozz is an influencer marketing, UGC and social media marketing agency in India. We connect brands with the right creators, produce UGC content, manage brand social media, and run influencer campaigns in India, the UAE, Saudi Arabia, the UK, Australia and Singapore." |

**Mechanism:** `buildMetadata()` gained an `absoluteTitle` option. Without it, a 50-character title would have been templated to "… | Kudozz — Kudozz". The homepage passes `absoluteTitle: true`, and every other page is unaffected (the default is `false`).

### Why "Best" is in the title
The title was chosen by the site owner on 17 Nov 2026 after reviewing the trade-off (report 01 §10):
- **For:** an exact match for the "best … agency (in) India" variants (≈1,000 impressions on "best" forms alone in the last export). It's short and leads with the primary keyword.
- **Against:** it reverses the 13 Nov 2026 removal, and it's a self-applied superlative (the ASCI code expects substantiation). Google's interpretation of "best" queries as list-intent doesn't change because of a title.
- **Mitigation:** "best" is not used in the H1, any heading or the body. The description frames it as the searcher's question. A comment in `src/app/page.tsx` records the rule.

## H3 changes

- Intro cards: "Social Media Management, Influencer Marketing, UGC and Content Creation" became "Influencer Marketing, UGC Marketing & Content, Social Media Marketing & Management". They were reordered to match the section order.
- Influencer section: the "UGC and content" category H3 was removed, since that content now lives in the UGC section.
- UGC section: 5 new H3s. Social section: 5 new H3s.
- Why Kudozz: "Creators across India's languages and cities" was replaced by "Your social media and creator work on one calendar", "A plan before a creator list" was broadened, and "Content made for each platform" was added. Regional sourcing is still covered in the India section and in FAQ answers.
- Process: steps 3–5 were renamed ("Find Creators and Content", "Plan and Execute", "Track, Analyse, Report"). **The duplicate set of 5 H3s was removed:** the desktop panels now use `<p role="heading" aria-level="3">`, so the HTML has one H3 per step and assistive tech on desktop still announces them as headings.
- FAQ: 12 question H3s (was 10). See report 04.
- Removed: the supporting-service cards "Creator Discovery" and "Campaign Management" (both still linked from the influencer section and Why Kudozz).
