## 3. How the homepage positions Kudozz today

| Service | Visibility on the homepage | Verdict |
|---|---|---|
| Influencer marketing | H1, title, a full section (`WhatWeDo`), the process, "Why Kudozz", India coverage and 7 of 10 FAQs | **Strong.** The page is overwhelmingly an influencer-marketing page. |
| Social media management | Title, H1, one card, 2 FAQs | **Moderate.** It's present but has no section of its own. |
| Social media *marketing* (phrase) | **0 occurrences** in the main content | **Gap.** The page never uses the phrase the third target cluster searches for. |
| UGC marketing | One card, one "Why Kudozz" card, 12 mentions of "UGC" in total, no section | **Weak.** Nothing explains what Kudozz's UGC is, how it differs from influencer posts, or what a brand gets. |

Word count of `<main>`: 2,213. Phrase counts: "influencer marketing" 24, "UGC" 12, "social media management" 4, "social media marketing" 0, "best" 1.

**Conclusion:** the homepage tells Google that Kudozz is an influencer marketing agency that also does social media. It doesn't adequately say that Kudozz offers UGC marketing or social media *marketing*, which are two of the three clusters this brief targets.

## 5. Keyword opportunities

1. **"best / top influencer marketing agency (in) India"**: about 1,500 impressions across variants, mostly position 5–12, served by the listicle. The homepage can compete for the non-list half of this demand (brands that want an agency, not a list). Matching the phrasing in the title and description should lift CTR wherever the homepage appears.
2. **"influencer marketing agency (in) India"**: about 1,200 impressions at position 12–15. This is the homepage's natural head term.
3. **UGC agency terms**: no page currently ranks, and the homepage should state the service clearly so it can support `/services/ugc-content-production`.
4. **Social media marketing agency India**: zero visibility. The homepage needs the phrase and a real section, and it should hand the depth to `/services/social-media-management`.

## 6. Content gaps

- No UGC section. The distinction between commissioned creator content and organic customer content isn't explained anywhere on the page.
- No social media marketing section. Platforms, scope and "paid ads scoped separately" only appear on the service page.
- No agency introduction that explains how the three services work together for a non-marketer.
- The process section only describes influencer campaigns.
- "Why Kudozz" only describes influencer campaigns ("over running creators in-house").
- The industries strip is a label plus chips, with no explanation.
- The FAQ covers influencer topics only. Nothing answers "influencer vs UGC", "what does a social media marketing agency handle?" or "how do I get started?".
- The final CTA mentions social presence only.

## 10. Metadata and H1 alternatives considered

### Title

| Option | Chars | Strengths | Weaknesses |
|---|---|---|---|
| **A. Best Influencer Marketing Agency in India \| Kudozz** | 50 | Exact match for the "best … agency in India" variants (≈1,500 impr). Short, so it won't truncate. Leads with the primary keyword. | A self-applied superlative. ASCI expects superlatives to be substantiated, and it reverses the 13 Nov decision. Doesn't name UGC or social media. |
| B. Influencer Marketing, UGC & Social Media Agency in India \| Kudozz | 65 | Names all three clusters with no claim to defend | Likely to truncate. Dilutes the primary term. "Social media agency" isn't the searched phrase. |
| C. Influencer Marketing & Social Media Agency in India \| Kudozz (current) | 60 | Measurable, since it has only been live since 13 Nov | No UGC, no "best" match, no change in CTR expected |

**Selected: A**, by decision of the site owner on 17 Nov 2026. I presented B as the alternative because of the superlative risk. A was chosen to target the "best" phrasing directly, with UGC and social media carried by the description, H1 and body. Mitigations: the H1 and body make no "best" claim, and the description frames "best" as the searcher's question.
