## Method

- **Inventory:** 925 posts at the start (926 after), searched by title, slug and full text; owner sections were read. Prior batches A-22 were checked, including batch 20 (home appliances and water purifiers) and batch 17 (distributors).
- **GSC:** the 2026-10-22 export (no query×page, date or country data).
- **Keyword volume:** not available.
- **Scores** (1-5) are editorial judgements, not forecasts. Columns:
  - **Int:** intent and relevance
  - **Lead:** brand-lead potential
  - **Dist:** distinctiveness
  - **Dem:** demand evidence
  - **Svc:** service relevance
