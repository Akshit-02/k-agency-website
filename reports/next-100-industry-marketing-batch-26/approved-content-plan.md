## Scoring method

Each criterion was scored 1–5 and weighted:

| Criterion | Weight | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Commercial relevance | 30% | How close the reader is to hiring an agency |
| Search intent and demand confidence | 25% | **Demand is unverified** (no keyword tool or Search Console in this session). This criterion scores only how clear and distinct the intent is, so treat it as a provisional score. |
| Relevance to Kudozz services | 20% | Direct fit with a `/services/*` page |
| Differentiation and usefulness | 15% | How little the existing library already says about it |
| Internal linking opportunity | 10% | Existing pages that can naturally link in and out |

## Article specification applied to all 15

- One H1 (the `title`), a separate `seoTitle` of 60 characters or fewer, and a unique `metaDescription` of 158 characters or fewer.
- A "Quick answer" section first, then H2 sections (with h3 where useful), and a `conclusion` H2 last.
- Tables, checklists or copy-ready templates where they help the reader.
- 4–6 FAQs.
- Every numeric example labelled hypothetical or illustrative. No client names, results, testimonials or quotes.
- **CTAs:** none are written into the body. The site's existing `blogCta.ts` and `ArticleBody` already insert exactly two CTAs: one mid-article at a section boundary (never in the first three blocks or just before the conclusion) and one at the bottom. Writing more into the body would break the "exactly two" rule.
- One natural inline link to the most relevant service page, plus 3+ contextual links to existing posts, and `related` slugs checked against the library.
- A hero SVG in the existing `/blog/brand-guides/` editorial card style, with descriptive alt text.
