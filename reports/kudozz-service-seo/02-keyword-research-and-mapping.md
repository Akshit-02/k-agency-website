# 02 · Keyword Research and Page Mapping

## Sources and rules
- **Real data:** `gsc-data.xlsx` (Queries sheet, top 1,000 queries, 5 Sep – 3 Nov 2026). Query numbers are **site-wide**; the export has no query × page split.
- **No volumes, difficulty scores or competitor rankings are stated.** No keyword tool is configured. Anything without a GSC number is labelled *unverified*.
- **SERP observations** come from the 17 Nov web searches in `reports/kudozz-homepage-seo/02` (US index, so treat them as directional). "Best … agency in India" SERPs are dominated by listicles, directories and sponsored PR, with some agency pages.
- **Owner decisions (17 Nov 2026):**
  1. The homepage keeps "Best Influencer Marketing Agency in India". The influencer pillar targets "Best Influencer Marketing **Services** in India".
  2. "Best" goes only on the core pages plus the sub-services with proven "[X] agency in India" demand. The others use natural "… Services in India" phrasing.
  3. H1s on "Best" pages are declarative.

### Why these choices
- **Influencer pillar = "services", not "agency":** the homepage already holds the agency title, and identical titles would split signals. "Influencer marketing services" has its own real demand (453 impressions at position 16, the best service-level opportunity in the export).
- **UGC marketing → `ugc-campaigns`, not `ugc-content-production`.** This **revises** the 17 Nov homepage report's suggestion. `ugc-campaigns` is the only UGC service page with any GSC visibility (120 impressions). Its scope (UGC strategy, creator sourcing, rights, ad-testing rounds) is what "UGC marketing" means, and the sitewide blog service link (`blogServiceLink.ts`) already sends UGC articles there. `ugc-content-production` keeps a distinct production intent ("UGC video production / content creation"), which matches how the homepage links it ("UGC content creation services").
- **Social media marketing + management share one page.** That page is the only one that delivers either service, and creating a duplicate "social media marketing" page was ruled out by the brief. One page targeting both phrases isn't cannibalisation. The copy states plainly that paid social is scoped separately, because "social media marketing" often implies ads.
- **No "Best" on 11 pages.** Barter, seeding, paid, affiliate, ambassador, reporting, strategy, discovery, social campaigns, LinkedIn and UGC production have no "agency in India" demand in GSC. Their searches are informational or "services" phrased, so a superlative would be unnatural there.

## 3. Questions brands ask before choosing (used for the new "What to look for" sections and FAQs)
- How do I choose an influencer / UGC / social media agency in India?
- What does the agency actually do, and what do I still do?
- How are creators found and vetted? (fake followers, audience location)
- Who owns the content, and can we use it in ads?
- How is performance measured, and what will the report show?
- What does it cost, and are creator fees shown separately?
- Is paid social included? (SMM)
- How often should we post? (SMM)
