## 1. Overlaps reviewed and how they were resolved

| # | Overlap | Evidence | Resolution | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Homepage vs `/services/influencer-marketing`** for "best influencer marketing agency in India" | The brief proposed identical titles. The homepage took this title on 17 Nov. The pillar has no GSC data yet (created 13 Nov). | **Owner decision:** the homepage keeps *agency* intent ("Best Influencer Marketing Agency in India"). The pillar H1 and copy describe what an engagement includes; the homepage describes the agency across all three services. The homepage links to the pillar 3 times with descriptive anchors, and the pillar's breadcrumb leads back to Home. | ✅ Done |

| 3 | **Social media marketing vs social media management** | Only one page delivers either service | Both keywords map to `/services/social-media-management`, so there's nothing to cannibalise. A new FAQ explains the difference, and paid social is stated as scoped separately. | ✅ Done |
