# 01 · Keyword and content strategy: Kudozz Discovery blogs 61–80

Prepared 26 Nov 2026. Search volumes, keyword difficulty and traffic estimates are **not** included. No keyword tool or API (DataForSEO, Ahrefs, Semrush) was connected for this batch, and the brief rules out fabricated figures. Keywords come from the topic brief, the GSC export in `gscReport/` (last 3 months, page level only, no query-to-page data for these URLs) and an understanding of how Indian brand buyers phrase these problems.

## Decisions that shaped the batch

1. **Upgrade, don't duplicate.** 14 of the 20 proposed topics already had a dedicated, indexed Kudozz URL (see 02). The site owner chose (26 Nov) to rewrite those posts in place to the batch-4 standard, keeping their slugs, `publishedAt` and inbound anchors. This is the same decision taken for batches 1–3.
2. **Six genuinely new URLs:** #62, #63, #72, #74, #78 and #79.
3. **#74 re-angled.** `/blog/influencers-for-product-launch` (Discovery #20) already covers launch discovery, so #74 targets **market entry** (new city, state or language market) on `/blog/influencers-for-market-entry` and links to #20 for launch mechanics.
4. **The owner chose to proceed despite the October indexing gap** (posts published after 3 Oct 2026 mostly had no impressions). 14 of the 20 URLs are already indexed with impressions, which lowers the risk for this batch.

## Cannibalisation pairs the brief flagged, and how each is split

| Pair | Split |
|---|---|
| Startups (#61) vs new brands (#78) | #61 covers the startup's operating constraints: founder time, runway, small rounds and a weekly sourcing habit. #78 is the one-page strategy document for any brand with no awareness, including a new label from an established company. They cross-link, and #61's `related` leads with #78. |
| Shopify (#63) vs ecommerce (#64) | #63 is product-level: which creator for which SKU, Shopify codes, UTMs and product pages. #64 is the year-round, multi-channel strategy: seasonal calendar, marketplaces and repeat creators. They link both ways. |
| Long-term partnerships (#77) vs always-on (#76) | #76 is the operating model for the whole programme: roster, discovery cadence and calendar. #77 is the individual deal: who to keep, what to offer and contract terms. Both also differ from `influencer-relationship-management` (#51, day-to-day care). |
| Product launches vs app launches | Launch discovery stays on #20 (`influencers-for-product-launch`). #73 covers apps (installs, activation, retention). #74 covers market entry. |
| Discovery vs strategy | Batch-4 posts are business-, industry- or objective-first and link out to batch 1–3 discovery how-tos, rather than repeating search steps in depth. |
