# 01 — Keyword and content strategy

Goal: bring brands, founders, marketing managers, D2C/ecommerce teams and agencies searching for ways to find creators to kudozz.in, and send the relevant ones to Kudozz Discovery (discover.kudozz.in).

## Method and limits

- Primary keywords are the ones in the brief. Secondary keywords and questions come from (a) the GSC export in `gscReport/Queries.csv` (5 Sep–17 Nov 2026) where kudozz.in already gets impressions, (b) the wording of the overlapping existing posts and (c) the questions the product actually answers (filters, profiles, lists, contact reveal).
- **No search volumes, difficulty scores or ranking forecasts are given**: no keyword tool data was available and none was invented. GSC impressions are quoted only where they exist.
- Live SERP review was limited to the competitor research for article #2 (official vendor sites). Other articles were angled from the existing library and GSC rather than a fresh SERP study, which is a gap worth closing with a keyword tool before the next batch.

## Cluster structure

- **Pillar:** #1 `find-indian-influencers` links down to every cluster; each article links back to it where natural.
- **Tools sub-cluster:** #2 (named tools) ↔ #5 (buyer criteria) ↔ #17 (tool vs manual vs agency).
- **Workflow sub-cluster:** #10 relevance → #7 research → #9 checks → #8 shortlist → #19 outreach list → #6 database, wrapped by #20 checklist.

## Product-fit notes (what the articles may say about Kudozz Discovery)

Kudozz Discovery is Instagram-only and India-only, so it is the natural next step in #1, #3, #4, #8, #10, #12, #14, #15, #16, #19 and #20, a fair "one option" in #2, #5, #17, and only a partial fit in #11 (not a UGC marketplace) and #18 (no YouTube coverage, stated explicitly in that article's CTAs).
