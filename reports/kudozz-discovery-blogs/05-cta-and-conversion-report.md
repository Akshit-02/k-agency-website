# 05 — CTA and conversion report

## Components

- **Mid-article** (`discovery-cta` body block → `DiscoveryArticleCTA variant="mid"`): restrained paper panel with a coral rule, product label, article-specific headline/body and an ink button. Hand-placed 40–60% through each article. Its presence switches off the automatic mid-article agency CTA, so each article has exactly two product CTAs.
- **End-of-article** (`discoveryCta` → `DiscoveryArticleCTA variant="end"`): ink panel with coral offset shadow and lime button, after the FAQ and immediately before Related Articles. It replaces the generic agency bottom CTA on these 20 posts and carries a quiet secondary agency path ("Prefer a team to plan and run the campaign? Talk to Kudozz" → `/for-brands?src=<slug>&pos=discovery_end&t=Kudozz Discovery#inquiry`, attributed by the existing inquiry form).
- The site-wide Discovery aside from the earlier integration is suppressed on these posts to avoid a third CTA.

Defaults (from the brief; body adjusted to name verified capabilities): headline "Find Creators Who Fit Your Next Campaign", button "Explore Kudozz Discovery". Most articles override the end body/headline to match the topic.

## Destination and tracking

`https://discover.kudozz.in/?utm_source=kudozz_website&utm_medium=blog&utm_campaign=discovery_content&utm_content=<slug>` built by `discoveryBlogHref(slug)` in `src/config/discovery.ts`. Verified on all 20 rendered pages (exact match). Opens in a new tab, `rel="noopener noreferrer"`, sr-only "(opens in a new tab)".

GA4 event via the existing `window.gtag` (`trackDiscoveryArticleClick` in `src/lib/discoveryAnalytics.ts`): `discovery_blog_cta_click` with `article_slug`, `article_title`, `cta_placement` (`mid_article` / `end_article`), `cta_label`, `source_page`, `destination_host`, `outbound`. No new analytics provider; no personal data.
