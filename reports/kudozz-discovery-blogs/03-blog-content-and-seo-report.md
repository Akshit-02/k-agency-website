## Shared on-page elements

- One H1 per page (verified on the rendered HTML); H2s from `heading` blocks feed the table of contents; H3s from `subheading` blocks.
- Canonical, Open Graph and Twitter metadata come from the existing `buildMetadata` (title = SEO title, description = meta description, OG image = the existing per-article `opengraph-image` route).
- Featured image: a custom editorial SVG diagram per article in `public/blog/discovery/` (2–8 KB each, 1600×700, house palette, descriptive `alt` in the post's `hero`).
- Dates: new posts `publishedAt 2026-11-25`; upgrades keep their original `publishedAt` and get `updatedAt 2026-11-25`; all show "Last reviewed November 2026".
- Byline: team author string; the site's existing `getPostAuthor` resolves it to the real Kudozz author for the category. No author, credential, rating or review data was invented.
- Tags and 3–4 hand-picked `related` slugs per post (cluster siblings first; none point at noindexed pages).
- #2 lists the official vendor URLs it verified in `mentions` (Article schema), with "Features checked in November 2026" on the page and no pricing.
