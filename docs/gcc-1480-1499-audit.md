## Material finding: the 2026 regional conflict

The research found that the 2026 Iran war (from 28 February 2026, still ongoing when checked) affected all six GCC states. Effects relevant to this cluster:

- Events were postponed: Informa moved Middle East Energy from April to September, and the Asian Cup draw slipped to May.
- The Esports World Cup 2026 moved from Riyadh to Paris.
- The UAE Attorney General warned that filming or resharing drone and missile incident footage is a criminal offence (The National, 6 March 2026).
- Dubai's events calendar had recovered by September (The National, 9 September 2026).

None of the existing ~45 GCC pages mentioned this. This batch adds it, with sources, to:

- the new events, gaming and 2027 pages
- the GCC risk register and GCC crisis sections
- the employee advocacy UAE section
- the UAE hub's permits section

**Not yet reviewed for conflict context:** the other UAE and Saudi pages, such as travel, restaurants and Ramadan. Recommended next review.
