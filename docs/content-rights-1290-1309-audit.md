## Legal and platform accuracy

All legal content is framed as general information, not legal advice, with a recommendation to take advice for specific agreements.

- **Copyright Act, 1957 (India), verified against the statutory text** (WIPO Lex consolidated copy; official source cited: India Code):
  - s.17: author is first owner; proviso (b) for a photograph, painting or portrait, engraving or cinematograph film made for valuable consideration at the instance of any person, that person is first owner in the absence of agreement to the contrary; proviso (c) employment.
  - s.19(1): assignment in writing, signed. s.19(5): no period stated → five years. s.19(6): no territory → India.
  - s.30: licence in writing; s.30A applies s.19 to licences with necessary adaptations.
  - The ownership page says how s.17(b) applies to a particular creator video is a legal question; it does not assert that brands own sponsored Reels.
- **Indian Contract Act, 1872, s.27** (restraint of trade) and **Percept D'Mark v Zaheer Khan (2006)**: described as courts being more cautious about restrictions beyond an agreement's term; practical step, keep exclusivity within the term. No enforceability guarantee given.
- **Corrections to existing pages:** ugc-content-usage-rights previously stated that the creator generally owns content unless ownership was transferred, as a general rule; it now distinguishes customer-posted content from commissioned content and points to the Indian defaults. The contract page's IP clause and the old usage-rights FAQ ("IP transfer is uncommon") were softened to what can be supported.
- **Platforms (as of October 2026, each with "confirm current steps"):**
  - Meta partnership ads: account-level vs post-level permission, partnership ad codes, pending delivery until approved (Meta developer documentation).
  - YouTube partnership ads powered by BrandConnect: creator links a video or accepts a brand request; Google states the advertiser must secure sufficient rights, possibly via a separate agreement (Google Ads Help).
  - TikTok Spark Ads: creator authorizes a post and shares a code with an authorization period; noted that TikTok remains blocked in India, so relevant only to campaigns aimed elsewhere.
- Example clause content is labelled "Example structure only, not a standard clause or legal advice". No "industry standard" periods, prices or percentages are claimed.
