## Facts checked (September 2026)

- CPA 2019: CCPA can penalise endorsers of misleading ads and prohibit endorsement for up to 1 year (3 years for repeat); due diligence defence. Guidelines for Prevention of Misleading Advertisements and Endorsements, 2022 (PIB, 9 June 2022). Penalty amounts deliberately not restated.
- CCPA advisory on promoting unlawful activities (PIB).
- ASCI: qualifications required and disclosed for technical health, nutrition and finance claims.
- SEBI: regulation amendments 29 Aug 2024; circular Oct 2024 on association with unregistered persons; clarifications Jan 2025.
- Trade Marks Act 1999 s.107 (false representation as registered); Nice classes; IP India e-filing. Fees and timelines not stated.
- Personality rights: Delhi High Court orders 2025–2026 (celebrities, AI deepfakes); described as case-by-case, not statutory.
- Indian Contract Act s.27 restraint of trade: framed generally with legal-advice caveat.
- Copyright term: life + 60 years (literary, dramatic, musical, artistic); 60 years from publication for films and sound recordings.
- YouTube comment moderation levels and blocked words; Instagram Hidden Words (help centres).
- No commission rates, tax rates, thresholds or fees are stated anywhere in the batch.
