## Research notes (2026-10-01)

No keyword tool or Search Console was available; no volumes or difficulty are claimed.

- 'influencer CPM / CPE / CPA': SERPs are formula guides that cover all three metrics on one page (Influencer Marketing Hub, Kolsquare, Lemniscate), which supports one consolidated page.
- 'reach vs impressions': SERPs explain the Instagram change to 'views'. Instagram replaced impressions and plays with views in April 2025 (Social Media Today), which is cited on the page.
- '% of marketing budget for influencers': results quote survey figures that conflict and mostly come from non-Indian samples, so the page explains a pilot-first approach instead of a percentage.
