Creator tiers

Nano or micro? Choose by job, not budget

Nano creators

  • Many authentic voices in a niche, city or language
  • Product seeding at volume
  • A low-cost way to learn who responds
  • More coordination to manage

Micro creators

  • More reach per partnership
  • More polished content
  • Fewer relationships to manage
  • More reliable delivery against dates
Many brands use both: nano to learn, micro to scale.
